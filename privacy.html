Privacy policy

Life OS engine · updated 17 September 2026

The Life OS engine is a single-user personal tool run by Mason Lowe for his own data only. It has no other users and collects nothing from anyone else. This policy describes what the application accesses from his own Google Account and what happens to it.

Google data the app requests

  • .../auth/calendar.readonly — read-only access to Google Calendar. Used to see events, their times and their descriptions, so that commitments with real dates can be tracked and prepared for. The app never creates, edits or deletes calendar entries under this scope.
  • .../auth/gmail.readonly — read-only access to Gmail. Used to notice things that create a commitment: a deadline in a course announcement, an application closing, a reply that is owed. The app reads message headers (subject, sender, recipient, date, list id), labels and Gmail's own short snippet. It does not store message bodies. Under this scope it never archives, deletes or modifies mail.
  • .../auth/gmail.send — send-only access to Gmail. Used for one thing: sending a message the user has approved himself, from a card in the app, with a second confirming tap. The app never sends mail on its own. This scope cannot read, archive, delete or modify mail.

Where the data goes

Into a private PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway, accessible only to the sole user. Other than the AI processing described below, it is not sold, rented, published, or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising or for any purpose other than showing that user his own next actions.

Short extracts — for example an email's sender, subject line and Gmail's preview text, or a calendar event's title, time, location and the start of its description — may be sent to AI models to classify or summarise them. These requests pass through OpenRouter, a model gateway, with prompt logging turned off, and are answered only by providers on a fixed approved list: currently Microsoft Azure, OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock, Google, DeepInfra, Fireworks, Together, Parasail, CoreWeave and NextBit. Each is based in the US or the EU, and none may train AI models on this data; some keep requests for a limited period under their API terms. No provider based in China is used. No credentials, and no full message bodies, are sent.

How long it is kept

  • Records of what was observed are kept as the app's long-term memory.
  • Any stored message content is deleted after 90 days.
  • Logs of model usage are kept for 12 months.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Withdrawing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently stops the app reading anything further.
